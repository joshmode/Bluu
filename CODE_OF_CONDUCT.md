# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique claims and code, not people.
- Label uncertainty honestly.
- Do not fabricate hardware results.
- Protect other people's private device and network information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private addresses, logs, or captures without consent.
- Deliberately misrepresenting codec support or test results.
- Pressuring contributors to violate codec licences.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or
ban participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally short and project-specific; it is compatible with the
spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
